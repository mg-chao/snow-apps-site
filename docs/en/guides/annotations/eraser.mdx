# Element, rectangle and brush erasers

Erasers clean up annotations. Decide whether to remove an entire element or restore only a local area, then choose a mode in **Eraser**.

## Remove a whole incorrect annotation

1. Open **Eraser** in the screenshot toolbar and choose **Element eraser**.
2. Move over the incorrect line, shape or text and press the left mouse button. Hold and drag across several annotations to remove the objects you hit.
3. Release, then check whether neighboring annotations were also removed.
4. Use **Undo** to restore objects removed by the last erasing gesture, then try a narrower gesture.

Element eraser deletes objects. Hitting a small part of a stroke can remove the entire stroke. Use Brush or Rectangle eraser when you want to preserve the rest.

## Restore a rectangular area

Choose **Rectangle eraser** and drag from one corner to the opposite corner. On release, the annotated area within the rectangle returns to the background. This suits a horizontal explanation or annotations in a larger area.

This restores background beneath current annotations. It does not understand and remove buttons, text or people contained in the original screenshot. To change source-image content, see [Filters and Smart erase](./filters.mdx).

## Erase locally with a brush

1. Choose **Brush eraser** and set its width, starting narrow.
2. Hold the left mouse button and trace the area to restore. Annotations under the brush are erased locally.
3. Release to finish the stroke, inspect what remains and add another stroke for missed areas.
4. If too much was erased, undo, reduce width and try again.

Width determines the area affected in each pass. Canvas zoom helps inspect edges; it does not change the configured eraser width or turn it into another tool.

## Continue editing after erasing

Use Undo first when you want to remove a background-restoration range or reverse erasure, rather than covering it again and creating more layers. Element deletion and local restoration behave differently; check the mode before continuing.

**Original text cannot be erased:** it belongs to source pixels. Element eraser targets editable elements. Use filters, Smart erase or a solid-filled shape instead.

**An entire line disappears:** Element eraser is active. Undo and use Brush eraser for local cleanup.

Deselect, inspect the restored area and copy or save the image. Related: [Brush](./brush.mdx) · [Filters](./filters.mdx) · [Element editing](./elements.mdx).
