# Groups, batch management, and recovery

Group pins by task to display related references together and recover closed content from management. **Currently visible windows**, **selected windows**, and **history records** are different scopes. Check menu counts before batch operations.

![Pin management: display an active group, filter and restore records, and distinguish closing from destruction](/images/guides/en/pin-management.svg)

*Instructional illustration. Closing may retain a record; destroying or deleting a record removes saved content.*

## Groups and visibility

**Move a window into a group**: right-click **Group: …** and choose an existing group to move the **current window** there. Choose **New group** to enter a name and create one. Use task-specific names such as “Error investigation” or “Design comparison.” Enter at most **16 characters** in **Group name** and click **Add**. Correct blank or duplicate names first. **Cancel** or closing the dialog abandons creation.

**Switch the displayed group**: open **Window group: …** in the system tray and select a group. Snow Shot saves the current group's state and restores applicable windows in the new group. The pin menu moves a window; the tray menu switches the active group. Check the menu title first.

**Organize the same group**: under **Pin to screen management** in a pin's context menu, use **Show all windows**, **Hide other windows**, **Close other windows**, or **Close all windows**. Check which task group is active.

Enable **Shake window to hide/show other windows** under **Settings → Pin to screen**, then quickly drag a pin back and forth to hide other windows in its group. When that group has hidden windows, the gesture can show them. Practice with two test pins; large cross-monitor movements are unnecessary.

Read group-deletion confirmations. Deleting a specified group also deletes its pin records, including closed windows. For **Delete empty groups**, “empty” can mean only closed records remain; those records are permanently removed too. The default group itself remains, but clearing it still removes its contents.

## Switch and restore with actions

Use the tray menu to choose a group directly, or assign **Switch window group** under **Global shortcuts**. A new configuration has no default binding; choose a combination you can comfortably hold.

1. Press the combination. The group preview appears and advances to the next candidate.
2. Keep its keys held and trigger the binding again to cycle candidates. Read the selected group name before releasing.
3. Release all keys involved in the shortcut to activate the previewed group. Press **Esc** while the preview remains active to cancel, or click the desired group in the preview to choose directly.

**Restore last closed window** on the same shortcut page restores the most recently closed ordinary pin in the **current group**. Defaults are **Ctrl+F3** on Windows and **Control+Shift+3** on macOS; existing configurations take precedence. If the expected pin is not restored, check the active group and retained records, then search management by date.

**Hide/show all windows** toggles the current group's visibility. Assign a shortcut and test hiding and showing two practice pins. Unlike closing or destroying, it does not require recreating content from files.

## Work with selected pins only

1. Show the non-live pins you need in the active group. Leave thumbnail, hide-at-top, and click-through modes. Hold **Ctrl (Command on macOS)** and **briefly click** each target to toggle its selection indicator. Do not drag during selection.
2. With at least two selected, right-click one of them to open the **selected-window** menu. Its scope differs from the current-group batch menu below. Check the selected count at the bottom.
3. To move them together, unlock first and drag a movable area of a selected pin. Drag an edge to resize together. Release to commit; press **Esc during the drag** to cancel that adjustment. Click a window's selection indicator to deselect it.

![Ctrl or Command short clicks toggle selection; a selected group moves together, while a single-pin modifier drag exports content](/images/guides/en/pin-gesture-select.svg)

*Instructional illustration. Select with short clicks, then choose between moving the group and exporting one pin.*

The selected menu's **Image stitching**, **Copy files to clipboard**, and **Save as file** affect selected windows only. **Group** moves them to an existing group; completing **New group** puts the selection in the new group. **Close other windows** retains the selection and closes other windows in the active group. **Close** or **Destroy** affects the selection; check its count first.

### Align and distribute the selection

Open **Align position** in the selected-window context menu. Alignment needs at least two selected windows and none locked. **Distribute horizontally** and **Distribute vertically** need at least three. Alignment moves windows without resizing them.

| Command | Result to inspect |
|---|---|
| Align left / Center horizontally / Align right | Match the left edge, horizontal center, or right edge of the selection's overall extent |
| Align top / Center vertically / Align bottom | Match the top edge, vertical center, or bottom edge of that extent |
| Distribute horizontally / Distribute vertically | Rearrange along that axis; check edge spacing. Insufficient space can still leave overlap |

When you no longer need group actions, deselect the windows with their indicators before opening an ordinary single-window menu.

## Batch-process current windows

Right-click **Pin to screen management → Batch actions**. The menu displays the number of eligible windows.

- **Select all** selects applicable visible pins so you can move or resize them together. A window's selection indicator can deselect it.
- **Image stitching** sends currently displayed images into the [Image collage](../image-editing/collage.mdx) workflow.
- **Copy files to clipboard** produces file-form output for destinations that accept pasted files.
- **Save as file** asks for an output folder. Wait, then check success and failure counts against the files.
- **Enable/disable thumbnail mode, hide at top, or lock mode** applies the setting to applicable visible windows.
- **Close / Destroy** respectively close windows or remove records. Batch-save material you need before either operation and distinguish the commands.

If a pin closes or becomes unreadable during processing, some outputs may succeed and others fail. Check the destination against the completion message and handle failed items individually.

## Restore from the management page

1. Open **Pin to screen management** in the main window. Records show source, group, and states such as Closed, Hidden, or Not closed.
2. Filter **Source** by screenshot, screenshot history, clipboard, selected files, and other sources; narrow **Date** with start and end values. Clear filters and refresh if a record seems absent.
3. Click **Restore** beside a closed record or **Show** beside a hidden one. Confirm the corresponding desktop pin appears.
4. **Select all** selects only the **current page**. Check counts before working across pages. Batch **Pin to screen** requires records from one group; reduce a mixed-group selection first.
5. Use **Delete** to remove records and read the confirmation. It removes saved records and corresponding open windows, unlike merely closing them.

## Closing and destruction

| Operation | Expected result |
| --- | --- |
| Hide | Temporarily removes the window from view; show it through its group or management |
| Close an ordinary pin | Ends the window; retained records can use management recovery |
| Destroy / Delete in management | Removes saved content; the confirmation describes the irreversible action |
| Automatic cleanup | Removes eligible closed records according to retention time, count, or disk limits |

Check permanent retention, days, count, and disk limits for pin history under **Settings → Export & storage**. Closed records are not a substitute for file archives; use Save as file for long-term images. Live windows depend on a running source and do not share every static-pin history recovery guarantee.
