# Take your first screenshot

Open the app you want to capture and show the entire target content. Practice with non-private text or a webpage. On Mac, complete [permission setup](./permissions.md) before the first capture.

![Snow Shot main window on macOS, with screenshot actions on the Global shortcuts page](/images/guides/start-main-interface-macos.webp)

*The image shows this computer's shortcut configuration. Use your own settings for customized bindings.*

## Select the content to retain

1. Start Snow Shot and press the capture shortcut: fresh defaults are Windows **F1** and macOS **Control+1**. Capture mode opens with magnified color and coordinate information near the pointer. Some Windows keyboards need **Fn+F1**. For customized bindings, use the value shown under **Global shortcuts** in the main window.
2. Place the pointer at the target area's upper-left corner. Hold the left button, drag to the lower-right corner, and release. The bright area is the retained selection; the capture toolbar appears beside it.
3. If the area is wrong, switch to **Move**, drag inside to reposition, or drag a border/corner to resize. Click the selection's dimension numbers to enter exact dimensions; see [precise selection](../capture/precise-selection.md).

## Add explanations and make corrections

1. Click **Arrow** on the capture toolbar and choose **Straight arrow** in the style bar. Drag from the explanation toward the target; release to keep the arrow on the canvas.
2. Click **Text**, click its position in the image, type a short explanation, and press **Esc** to finish typing. The text stays on the image; you can now switch tools to continue annotating.
3. Click **Undo** for a mistake. To move an annotation, switch to **Select elements**, click the object, and drag after its control points appear. Moving a selection and moving an annotation use different tools.

See [arrows](../annotations/arrows.mdx) for curvature, endpoint symbols, and later editing, or [text annotations](../annotations/text.mdx) for font size, stroke, and layout.

## Choose where the result goes

| Goal | Toolbar action | Check afterwards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Send to a chat or document | **Copy to clipboard**, then paste in the destination app | The pasted image includes annotations within the selection. |
| Keep a local file | **Save as file**, choose path and format, then save | Open the file and inspect its content and edges. |
| Keep a desktop reference | **Pin to screen** | The image floats above other windows. |
| Discard this attempt | **Cancel screenshot** or Esc | Capture closes without exporting this result. |

For selecting and copying directly, use the global **Copy to clipboard** action: fresh defaults are Windows **Ctrl+F1** and macOS **Control+2**. It copies after selection confirmation, unlike the ordinary screenshot workflow with its full editing toolbar.

## First-use troubleshooting order

If the shortcut does nothing, confirm Snow Shot is running, then check Fn, conflicts, and global-shortcut state. If capture opens but the image is wrong, check screen-recording permission on Mac. On Windows, inspect the capture method in screenshot settings; see [basic capture](../capture/basics.mdx).

Retrieve an earlier capture from [screenshot history](../output/history.md). History and exported files are independent: deleting an exported image does not clear its history record.
